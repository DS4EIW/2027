DS4EIW 2027 - Call for Papers

Topics of Interest

DS4EIW connects technical research with societal impact. The workshop brings together researchers and practitioners from data management, machine learning, AI ethics, and the social sciences to explore practical approaches to building more inclusive data systems and AI applications.

We focus on transparency, fairness, and accountability throughout the data lifecycle, from collection and storage to processing and analysis. We welcome database techniques for bias detection and mitigation, fair prediction methods, and system architectures supporting explainability, transparency, and auditability.

We invite research papers, experience reports, and demonstration proposals, including work in progress. Contributions should consider diversity across dimensions such as gender, ability, age, ethnicity, and religion and, where applicable, provide empirical evidence of their impact on equity and inclusion.

Methodologies and Concepts

  • Concepts, methodologies, and solutions for modelling, managing, and analysing human behaviour and activity
  • Algorithmic accountability
  • Explainable algorithms
  • Machine learning for bias mitigation
  • Fairness-focused machine learning applications
  • Trustworthy AI: From principles to practices
  • Data analytics and artificial intelligence algorithms
  • Recommendations for people, analysts, and decision-makers
  • Recommendations for policymakers
  • Intelligent systems to enhance user awareness of algorithm decisions
  • Ethical issues of data collection, storage, and exchange
  • Interactive query refinement and data processing
  • Innovative solutions for exploring, analysing, and visualising data
  • User-controlled algorithms for data integration, cleaning, and analysis
  • Perception-aware data processing and analytics
  • Database systems designed for highly interactive and inclusive applications

Application Scenarios

  • Education
  • Employment and workplace equity
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Healthcare systems and personalised medicine
  • Public safety and smart cities
  • Citizens' mobility and transportation
  • Urban economy and environmental planning
  • Data-driven journalism
  • Gender equality and diversity initiatives
  • Inclusive language processing
  • Social services, unemployment, and homelessness
  • Economic, social, and personal development
  • Government transparency and accountability
  • Analysis of user-generated content (tweets, microblogs, reviews, etc.)
  • Pandemic response and management (e.g., COVID-19)
  • Gender Equality Plans and fairness in database systems
  • Equity-focused applications in public and private sectors
